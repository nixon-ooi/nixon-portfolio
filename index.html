About Me

"

I'm a UX designer who works at the intersection of research, wireframing and prototyping, rolling up my sleeves to understand people first, then designing with them in mind. I thrive in collaborative product teams where honest critique and good process push the work further.

A background in accounting gave me a strong foundation in systems thinking and structured problem-solving, but my real passion was always shaping the human experience. UX design is where that clicked.

In this era of AI and rapid change, I'm drawn to the space where technology meets people, working to make that relationship feel less overwhelming and more human.

Because technology enables, but design establishes.

"
